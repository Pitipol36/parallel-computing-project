BVH Ray Tracing of Large Triangle Meshes

Sequential CPU vs CUDA, on up to a million triangles

Pitipol Tangcherdchar
Parallel Computing · 5 November 2026

Hi, I’m Pitipol. My project ray-traces big 3D-scanned meshes, up to about a million triangles, and compares a tuned single-threaded CPU version against four CUDA versions. The image on the right is the Stanford dragon, 871 thousand triangles, rendered by the GPU in about 7 milliseconds.
