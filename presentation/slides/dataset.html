The models come from the Stanford 3D Scanning Repository, a standard public dataset in graphics research. The bunny is small and good for development. The dragon, at 871 thousand triangles, is the main benchmark model. The Happy Buddha, at 1.09 million, is the largest one I could use: Stanford’s server, which is the only source for the 28-million-triangle Lucy, could not be reached, so the plan’s fallback applies. There is also a small procedural sphere-on-ground scene for unit tests, so tests never need a download.
