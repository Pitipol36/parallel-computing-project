Where the time goes

The CPU BVH build (0.9 s on the Buddha) now takes longer than upload + render + download.

Small or wide blocks (8×8, 32×8) win slightly: nearby pixels trace more coherent paths.

13 · 1920×1080, 4 AO; block sizes measured on the dragon

Two more observations. On the left, once the render takes only a few milliseconds, the single-threaded BVH build on the CPU becomes the biggest cost by far: almost a second on the Buddha, against under 30 milliseconds for upload, kernel and download combined. On the right, block-size tuning: 8 by 8 and 32 by 8 are a little faster than 16 by 16 or 32 by 32, because pixels close together in a block take similar paths through the tree, which means less divergence.
