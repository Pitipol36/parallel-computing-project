To win back the quality, I built two higher-quality GPU builders. PLOC clusters bottom-up: starting from single triangles in Morton order, every cluster looks at its eight neighbours on each side and finds the one whose merged box is smallest; mutual nearest neighbours merge, and a scan I wrote myself, the same pattern as the scan lab, compacts the survivors. Its tree is 10 percent above SAH cost, and it builds the Buddha in 14 milliseconds. TRBVH starts from the LBVH and improves it: under every large node it grows a treelet of seven subtrees and runs a small dynamic program over all 127 subsets to find the best possible shape for that treelet. On the GPU a whole warp cooperates on each treelet, which made it 9 times faster than one thread per treelet. Its tree is within 5 percent of the SAH builder, in 59 milliseconds. For both, the GPU tree is identical to the CPU version, which is how I verified them.
