Correctness is checked automatically with a --verify flag. For every pixel, the primary ray must hit the same triangle ID on the CPU and GPU. On the dragon only 7 of 307 thousand pixels differ, and those sit exactly on triangle edges, where the GPU’s fused multiply-add rounds differently. Ties at equal distance go to the smaller triangle index, so traversal order never changes the answer. The images match at 56.7 dB, and on the procedural test scene every pixel hits the same triangle.
