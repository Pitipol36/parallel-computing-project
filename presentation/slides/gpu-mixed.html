Two more things changed in the second round. First, scanned meshes are the easiest possible input, because all their triangles are about the same size. So I added a harder scene with mixed sizes: eight huge ground triangles, the dragon, bunnies at three very different scales, and thin pillars. Second, binned SAH is itself an approximation, so I added a full-sweep SAH reference that tries every split on every axis, and made that 100 percent. On the scans the binned builder is within one percent of it, but on the mixed scene it is 11 percent worse. The mixed scene separates the GPU builders much more: the LBVH is 82 percent above the reference, PLOC and H-PLOC about 20, and TRBVH only 3 percent, which means it beats the CPU SAH builder on this scene while building 19 times faster. I also made the timing fairer: a warm-up, eleven runs interleaved across builders, and medians with the interquartile range. The clocks can't be locked without administrator rights, so quality claims rest on SAH cost and traversal counters, which are deterministic.
