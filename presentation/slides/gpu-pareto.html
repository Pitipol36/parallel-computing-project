20 · Render = 1-thread CPU render time on each tree, 1080p, 4 AO. Quality order matches both papers: LBVH < PLOC < TRBVH < SAH.

Here are all the builders on one chart: build time against tree quality, lower left is better. Every GPU builder sits on the front. The LBVH is the fastest by far but its tree is 18 to 28 percent worse. PLOC takes two to three times as long and halves that loss. TRBVH takes another three to four times as long and gets within 5 percent of the SAH builder, while still building fifteen times faster than it. The ordering matches what both papers report. On rendering, the TRBVH tree is only 6 percent slower than the SAH tree on the Buddha. So the choice depends on how many rays a tree will serve: the LBVH for previews and anything that moves, TRBVH when the tree will be used for many frames. The CPU SAH tree only pays for its build after hundreds to thousands of full-HD frames.
