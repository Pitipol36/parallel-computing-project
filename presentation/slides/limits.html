Limitations and future work

Faster high-quality builds

TRBVH still grows and rewires each treelet on one lane; splitting the largest subsets across lanes would cut its 59 ms further.

Sort AO rays

Grouping rays by direction octant would make paths within a warp more coherent and cut divergence.

Larger meshes

Lucy (28M triangles, ~1.8 GB on the GPU) was unreachable; it would test the memory-bound trend further.

Profile the traversal

Nsight Compute on registers, occupancy and local-memory stack traffic; benchmark on an idle GPU.

A few limitations and next steps. The BVH is now built on the GPU with three builders; the TRBVH build could go faster still, because one lane still grows and rewires each treelet and the largest subsets are solved by a single lane. Sorting AO rays by direction would make warps more coherent. I could not get the 28-million-triangle Lucy model, which would push the memory-bound trend further. And I would profile the traversal in Nsight Compute and rerun the benchmarks on a completely idle GPU to tighten the numbers.
