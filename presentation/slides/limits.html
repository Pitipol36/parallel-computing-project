Limitations and future work

Better GPU-built trees

The GPU LBVH is done; PLOC and TRBVH would win back its 21–30 % SAH-cost loss at a fraction of the CPU build time.

Sort AO rays

Grouping rays by direction octant would make paths within a warp more coherent and cut divergence.

Larger meshes

Lucy (28M triangles, ~1.8 GB on the GPU) was unreachable; it would test the memory-bound trend further.

Profile the traversal

Nsight Compute on registers, occupancy and local-memory stack traffic; benchmark on an idle GPU.

A few limitations and next steps. The BVH is now built on the GPU, but the LBVH tree is 21 to 30 percent worse by SAH cost, so the next step is a higher-quality parallel builder such as PLOC or TRBVH. Sorting AO rays by direction would make warps more coherent. I could not get the 28-million-triangle Lucy model, which would push the memory-bound trend further. And I would profile the traversal in Nsight Compute and rerun the benchmarks on a completely idle GPU to tighten the numbers.
