Limitations and future work

Build the BVH on the GPU

An LBVH from Morton codes, radix sort and scan would remove the now-dominant 0.9 s CPU build.

Sort AO rays

Grouping rays by direction octant would make paths within a warp more coherent and cut divergence.

Larger meshes

Lucy (28M triangles, ~1.8 GB on the GPU) was unreachable; it would test the memory-bound trend further.

Profile the traversal

Nsight Compute on registers, occupancy and local-memory stack traffic; benchmark on an idle GPU.

14 · BVH ray tracing on CUDA

A few limitations and next steps. The BVH build is single-threaded on the CPU and now dominates total time, so a GPU LBVH built with Morton codes, sorting and scan is the clear next step. Sorting AO rays by direction would make warps more coherent. I could not get the 28-million-triangle Lucy model, which would push the memory-bound trend further. And I would profile the traversal in Nsight Compute and rerun the benchmarks on a completely idle GPU to tighten the numbers.
