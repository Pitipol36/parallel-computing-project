RTX 2060 (30 SMs) vs Core i7-9700F, one thread. Kernel time, median of 5 runs.

Here is the headline. On every setting I measured, the GPU is between 35 and 180 times faster than the tuned single-threaded CPU, using the same BVH, the same rays and the same shading code. The low end is the naive kernel; the optimized kernels sit between about 90 and 180 times. The next slides show how that number moves with mesh size, resolution and ray count.
