Speedup vs ray workload

The gap widens with more rays: 47× at 512² to 145× at 4K. The GPU needs many rays in flight to hide memory latency, and the fixed upload cost is shared by more pixels (end to end 11× → 79×).

11 · Dragon (871K triangles); left 4 AO; right 1080p. Repeated runs vary by about ±25 %.

This is where the expected trend does show up: as the number of rays grows, so does the GPU’s lead. At 512 by 512 the GPU is 47 times faster; at 4K it is 145 times faster. With few rays the GPU cannot keep enough warps busy to hide memory latency, and end to end the fixed upload cost dominates. With more AO samples the picture is similar, but noisier: the desktop had background programs using the GPU, so repeated runs of the same setting varied by about 25 percent, and I would not read too much into the dip at 4 AO.
