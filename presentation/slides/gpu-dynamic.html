19 · 1280×720, 4 AO; update + render per frame. Refit keeps the frame-0 topology and only recomputes boxes (0.9 ms).

Where a fast build really matters is a scene that changes every frame. Here the dragon explodes and every triangle flies off in its own random direction. The cheapest update is a refit: keep the tree, recompute the boxes. It takes under a millisecond, but triangles that started as neighbours end up far apart, so the old boxes stretch across the whole explosion. The tree's SAH cost grows more than a thousandfold and the last frame takes 41 seconds to render. Rebuilding on the CPU keeps the tree good but costs a second per frame. Rebuilding with the GPU LBVH costs 4.6 milliseconds, and the whole frame including the render takes 20 milliseconds: 50 frames per second.
