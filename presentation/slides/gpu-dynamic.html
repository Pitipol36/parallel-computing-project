21 · 1280×720, 4 AO; update + render per frame. Refit keeps the frame-0 topology and only recomputes boxes (1.1 ms).

Where a fast build really matters is a scene that changes every frame. Here the dragon explodes and every triangle flies off in its own random direction. The cheapest update is a refit: keep the tree, recompute the boxes. It takes about a millisecond, but triangles that started as neighbours end up far apart, so the old boxes stretch across the whole explosion. The tree's SAH cost grows a thousandfold and the last frame takes 40 seconds to render. Rebuilding on the CPU keeps the tree good but costs a second per frame. Rebuilding on the GPU every frame keeps every tree close to SAH quality: the LBVH runs at 57 frames per second, PLOC at 38 and TRBVH at 17, and TRBVH's tree stays within one percent of the CPU SAH rebuild. Once the pieces are scattered, PLOC's advantage over the LBVH disappears, so for this scene the fastest builder wins.
