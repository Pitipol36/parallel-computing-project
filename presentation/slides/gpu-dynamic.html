Where a fast build really matters is a scene that changes every frame. Here the dragon explodes and every triangle flies off in its own random direction. The cheapest update is a refit: keep the tree, recompute the boxes. It takes about a millisecond, but triangles that started as neighbours end up far apart, so the old boxes stretch across the whole explosion. The tree's SAH cost grows a thousandfold and the last frame takes 47 seconds to render. Rebuilding on the CPU keeps the tree good but costs about a second per frame. Rebuilding on the GPU every frame keeps every tree close to SAH quality: the LBVH runs at 56 frames per second, H-PLOC at 44, PLOC at 37 and TRBVH at 17, and TRBVH's tree stays within one percent of the CPU SAH rebuild. Once the pieces are scattered, PLOC's and H-PLOC's advantage over the LBVH disappears, so for this scene the fastest builder wins.
